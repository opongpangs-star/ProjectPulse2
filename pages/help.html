Frequently asked questions

How do I check my project's Pulse status?

Every team has a Project Pulse shown on the Dashboard and Projects page as a colored badge with an icon and a short label — never just a color or a bare number. Click through to a team's workload page to see the specific reasons behind the current Pulse.

What do the four Pulse levels mean?

  • ⬆️ Strong — the team is ahead of schedule.
  • ➡️ Steady — the team is on track.
  • ⬇️ Weak — the team is behind schedule and may need attention.
  • ⏸️ Dormant — there has been no recent activity logged for the team.

Pulse is always computed from real activity — submissions, task updates, and deadlines — so a level is never assigned arbitrarily.

How do I submit work for review?

From your team's workload page, open the current milestone and use the submission action to attach your work and mark it ready. This places the submission into your advisor's Review Queue, where you can track its status as it moves from "submitted" to "under review" to "feedback ready."

How does the Review Queue ordering work?

The queue is ordered automatically using three factors: how long a submission has been waiting, how urgent its deadline is, and how much risk the associated project currently carries. Advisors can manually re-order an item ahead of the queue, but only after entering a reason, which is then shown alongside the item so the ordering stays transparent.

User Guide

A quick tour of the main areas

ProjectPulse looks a little different depending on whether you sign in as a student or a lecturer (advisor), but the same main areas apply to both roles.

Dashboard

Your starting point after signing in. Students see their team's current milestone, progress, and any notifications that need attention. Lecturers see a set of stat tiles, a Risk Radar highlighting teams that need attention, and their personal Review Queue — a prioritized list of submissions still waiting to be reviewed.

Projects

A searchable, filterable grid of every project a lecturer supervises (or, for a student, their own team's project). Use it to scan Pulse status, progress, and risk across teams at a glance, then open any project for its full detail and workload breakdown.

Review Queue

Where advisors work through submitted milestones. The queue is ordered by wait time, urgency, and risk, with the reasoning always visible — an advisor can manually re-prioritize an item, but only by providing a reason first. Opening an item takes you to the Review Workspace to leave structured feedback.

Calendar

A combined view of milestone deadlines, review dates, and team availability. Students use it to plan working sessions around deadlines and teammates' free time; lecturers use it to see upcoming deadlines across all the teams they supervise.

Notifications

Time-sensitive updates such as feedback being ready, a deadline approaching, or a teammate flagging a blocker. Notifications are grouped by severity so the most urgent items are never buried.

Profile

Your account details, team membership (for students) or advisee list (for lecturers), and any personal preferences for the app.

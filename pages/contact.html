Get in touch

ProjectPulse is a student project built for a course at the University of Phayao, not a supported commercial product — so there isn't a formal support desk behind this page. That said, feedback, bug reports, and suggestions are genuinely welcome.

How to reach us

If you're reviewing this prototype as part of a course, please reach out via your course's usual channels (e.g. the class discussion board or your instructor's contact details) rather than through this page.

Found something broken?

Since this is a static demo without a backend, most issues are reproducible simply by describing what you clicked and what you expected to happen — that's usually enough detail to track down and fix.

For general questions about how the app works, the Help Center and User Guide are a good place to start first.
